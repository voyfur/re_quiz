Checked on 2026-11-06 while auditing package quantities, component details, and caffeine tags in the approved 206-set analysis. This note records source facts and unresolved listing fields; it does not turn seller marketing into independent product claims.

- Set 111 (`RE11494`): Race Expert lists two packs of ЇDLO navy-style pasta. The manufacturer gives 90 g net weight and a 350 g prepared serving per pack. Two packs therefore weigh 180 g net in total. Sources: [Race Expert set](https://raceexpert.com.ua/ru/nabor-pohodnaya-eda-yidlo-20), [ЇDLO manufacturer product page](https://idlo.com/ru/makarony-po-flotsky-yidlo/).

Sources: [Race Expert set](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6099-nabir-formula-spokoyu-1-554-grn-nabir-1112-gribi-ta-ada), [linked Race Expert product](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6335-muhomor-chervonij-drumnbads-500-mg-60-kapsul-0-grn-re53).

Race Expert product cards identify Magnesium Bisglycinate as 60 capsules and Multi Vita as 60 tablets; the Omega-3 pack size was not confirmed. Sources: [Race Expert set](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6163-nabir-vnutrishnya-rivnovaga-2-046-grn-nabir-1116), [Magnesium Bisglycinate](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6403-magnij-bisglicinat-nutriversum-magnesium-bisglycinate-6), [Multi Vita](https://raceexpert.com.ua/vitamini-nutriversum-multi-vita-60-tabletok).

The set page does not state pack multipliers for either supplement. Sources: [Race Expert set](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6097-zmenshennya-stresu-ta-regulyaciya-snu-2-632-grn-nabir-1), [Race Expert Reishi product](https://raceexpert.com.ua/5478-rejshi-mind-shi-reishi-mushroom-1000-mg-60-kapsul-73373), [Mind Shi manufacturer](https://www.mindshi.com/cbd-oil-isolate-1000mg-orange).
- Set 175 (`Nabir_1111`): the linked DRUMn’BADS Lion’s Mane 700 mg retail product is a 60-capsule pack. The set page does not state a pack multiplier. Sources: [Race Expert set](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6098-nabir-koncentraciya-ta-yasnist-1-554-grn-nabir-1111-gri), [linked Race Expert product](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6780-yizhovik-grebinchastij-drumnbads-700-mg-60-kapsul-48202).

The Race Expert FLOW product page specifies 200 mg caffeine per 60 ml shot; Nutriversum's PWO Shot page also specifies 200 mg per 60 ml; Nutriversum identifies AAKG Shot as caffeine-free. The 400 mg per set tag is a calculation from the two separately listed caffeinated shots, one each as listed on the set page. The OstroVit creatine pack mass is not shown. Sources: [Race Expert set](https://raceexpert.com.ua/index.php?product_id=7151&route=product%2Fproduct), [Race Expert FLOW PWO Shot](https://raceexpert.com.ua/6583-peredtrenuvalnij-napij-nutriversum-flow-pwo-shot-grejpf), [Nutriversum PWO Shot](https://nutriversum.com/pwo-shot-bubblegum), [Nutriversum AAKG Shot](https://nutriversum.org/pre-Workout-formulas/DARK_-_AAKG_SHOT/).
