## 3. Deep Domain & Architectural Analysis of Critical Flaws
### Finding 1: Gross Non-Food Leaks into 'Food & Snacks' (CRITICAL FAIL)
> [!CAUTION]
> **11 out of 38 catalog candidates in `food` are NOT food sets:**
> In `quiz-set-audit.json`, numerous non-food kits were erroneously assigned `candidate_tiers.component_based: ['portable_food_snacks']` or `seller_positioned: ['portable_food_snacks', 'endurance_energy']`.
> As a result, when a customer asks for snacks, the quiz recommends:
> 1. **RE89489 («Основа регулярних тренувань»):** A **135×70 cm cotton bath towel**, Myprotein shaker, and Nutriversum daily vitamins bundled with a 3-month gym club membership. Zero food items!
> 2. **RE11665 («Активний рух»):** A **speed jump rope with counter**, double ab wheel roller with knee pad, and massage ball. Gym equipment sold as food!
> 3. **RE11663 («Йога та пілатес»):** An **EasyFit yoga mat** and textile resistance band. Fitness gear sold as edible snacks!
> 4. **Nabir_1110 («Зменшення стресу та регуляція сну»):** 60 capsules of Mind Shi Reishi mushroom nootropics and 60 capsules of Sleep Deep sleep medication. Sleeping pill bottles sold as food!
> 5. **RE11688 («Набір маси»):** 1,000g Kevin Levrone Anabolic Mass powder and 300g Creatine Monohydrate. A 1.3kg bodybuilding powder tub sold as 'portable snacks'!
> 6. **RE88934 («Регулярний зал»):** 500g Creatine Monohydrate tub, gym shaker, and socks.
> 7. **RE11019 («Щоденний комфорт»):** White socks size 40–42, shaker, glass water bottle, and keychains.
> 8. **RE11026 («Спортивний подарунок»):** 2 pairs of socks, 3 bullet keychains, and a bottle of Vitamin D3 softgels.
> 9. **RE11651 («Нічний ZMA»):** 90 capsules of ZMA sleep minerals with 1 bar.
> 10. **RE11632 («Розум і Сила»):** 60 capsules of Mind Shi 4Mushroom nootropics with cookies.
> 11. **RE11660 («Марафонець»):** 300g Creatine Monohydrate tub bundled with 5 carbohydrate gels.

### Finding 2: The Dehydrated Camping Meal Dead-End (100% Failure Rate)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> When an outdoor camper or hiker selects the officially advertised option `component_class: 'dehydrated_meal'` ('Сублімована туристична їжа'), the quiz engine returns **`status: no_match` (0 results)**.
> **Root Cause:** In `quiz-set-audit.json`, the genuine camping ration sets (`RE11494` ЇDLO 2.0, `RE11495` ЇDLO Club, `RE88977` ЇDLO 3.0) were tagged with `component_classes: ['protein_carbohydrate_food']` instead of `'dehydrated_meal'`. Meanwhile, `COMPONENT_CLASSES_BY_GOAL['food']` offers `'dehydrated_meal'` as a primary steering filter. Every hiker who clicks this button experiences a dead-end blank screen.

### Finding 3: Complete Absence of Sweet vs Savory Taste Profiling
> [!WARNING]
> The Race Expert food catalog contains two diametrically opposed taste worlds:
> - **Sweet confectionary:** Sweet protein & carb bars (LipoBar, Fitly, Myprotein cookies), fruit crisps, and sweet dessert shakes.
> - **Savory / Salty meat snacks:** 14 sets of dried meat jerky (РябChick dried chicken slices in soy sauce, honey mustard, paprika, Gouda cheese; М'яско ToGo beef, pork, turkey, chicken).
> **The Problem:** The quiz interface and data model contain **zero fields, signals, or questions for taste or flavor preference** (sweet vs savory / salty / meat).
> A hiker or cyclist specifically demanding salty meat jerky for salt replenishment during sweaty endurance workouts is indiscriminately fed chocolate bars, cookies, or sublimated strawberries. Conversely, a sweet tooth buyer gets meat jerky. Furthermore, sets like RyabChick have `product_forms: []` empty in their audit record, causing them to fall into 'unknown' when `product_form: 'bars'` or `'food_or_meal'` is requested.

### Finding 4: Deceptive Marketing Claims: '(без сторонніх речей)'
> [!CAUTION]
> In `draft_matcher.py:_build_why_suitable()`, when `component_class: 'protein_carbohydrate_food'` is matched, the engine generates:
> *«Категорія товарів: у наборі саме Спортивне харчування та батончики (без сторонніх речей).»*
> In reality, the recommended set frequently contains:
> - A 135×70 cm cotton bath towel (`RE89489`)
> - 4 pairs of athletic socks (`RE11026`)
> - 60 capsules of nootropic mushrooms or sleeping pills (`RE11632`, `RE11651`)
> - A mandatory 3-month gym club membership (accounting for a large portion of the 1500 UAH price).
> Claiming '(без сторонніх речей)' ('without extraneous items') when the bundle is dominated by non-food accessories is false advertising that destroys customer trust.

### Finding 5: Artificial Validator Wall Blocking Gym & Fitness Athletes
> [!IMPORTANT]
> `draft_matcher.py` restricts `ACTIVITY_OPTIONS_BY_GOAL['food'] = {'running', 'cycling', 'hiking'}`.
> If a gym member, crossfitter, or fitness enthusiast enters the quiz looking for protein bars and selects `fitness`, `fitness_gym`, or `strength_training`, the validator raises an unhandled `DraftMatchError: activity is not supported for the selected need`.
> Yet in the catalog, almost all protein bars (LipoBar, Fitly, Myprotein) and meal shakes explicitly list `fitness_gym` in their source metadata! The engine crashes on the very audience that consumes the most protein bars.

### Finding 6: Low Budget Floor Poisoning (< 1000 UAH)
> [!NOTE]
> The entire catalog has **ZERO food sets below 1149 UAH**.
> When a buyer specifies a budget of 400–1000 UAH, the engine triggers `near_miss` or `floor_anchor`. The two lowest-priced sets matched to `food` are:
> 1. `RE11688` (1149 UAH) — 1,000g Anabolic Mass powder tub + 300g Creatine Monohydrate.
> 2. `RE11632` (1215 UAH) — Mind Shi 4Mushroom nootropic capsules.
> Consequently, every budget-conscious buyer looking for affordable snack bars is told: *'Here is our lowest-priced food anchor: a 1kg tub of bodybuilder gainer powder and mushroom brain pills!'*.

### Finding 7: High Budget Upsell Ceiling (1800–3500 UAH)
> [!NOTE]
> The maximum price of any food set in the catalog is **1500 UAH**.
> High-budget buyers (ultra runners, expedition teams, corporate clients) willing to spend 2000–3500 UAH are shown standard 1500 UAH sets with club memberships, because the store has no large bulk snack boxes (e.g. 50-pack bar boxes or multi-month expedition ration crates).

### Finding 8: The Running & Cycling Activity Blackout for Meat Jerky & Meal Shakes (24 Unintended Failures)
> [!CAUTION]
> **Runner & Cyclist Blackout:** Every single savory meat snack set (12 sets of М'яско ToGo and 2 sets of RyabChick) lists `activity_groups: ['fitness_gym', 'general_lifestyle', 'hiking', 'walking']`. **None of them list `running` or `cycling` in their metadata!**
> Similarly, the liquid meal replacement shakes (`RE11557` Napiy, `RE11695` Мандрівний смаколик) only list `hiking` or `fitness_gym`.
> Meanwhile, `ACTIVITY_OPTIONS_BY_GOAL['food']` in `draft_matcher.py` strictly forces `activity` to be one of `{'running', 'cycling', 'hiking'}`.
> Because `activity` is treated as a hard gate CORE field in `draft_matcher.py:_candidate_fit` (`elif key in CORE_ANSWER_FIELDS: return None`), selecting `activity: 'running'` or `'cycling'` **completely eliminates 100% of the store's meat snacks and meal shakes**.
> And because selecting `activity: 'fitness_gym'` or `'walking'` triggers a `DraftMatchError` crash, it is practically impossible for any runner, cyclist, gym athlete, or walker to find meat snacks through the quiz unless they guess to select `hiking` or `other`.
